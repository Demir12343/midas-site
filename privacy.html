Privacy Policy

Midas Bot is a Discord bot that links a member's Epic Games account to their Discord account.

What we collect

When you sign in with Epic Games, we receive your Epic account ID and display name. We store them together with your Discord user ID.

How we use it

We use this information only to verify your account, assign a role in the Discord server, and show Fortnite statistics on your request. We do not sell or share it with third parties.

Deletion

To remove your data, contact the server administrators on Discord and ask to be unlinked.
